HORIZON INDEX / HISTORY

World History

The major confirmed events that shaped the world of Horizon.

~2,000 YEARS BEFORE BLAISE

The First Spawn arrive

Five gigantic beasts called the First Spawn crawl from the oceans and devastate civilization, leaving fewer than one billion people alive.

AFTER THE DEVASTATION

The Divergence

The 3rd Guardian tears Earth into islands and archipelagos.

LATER

The First Spawn fall

The First Spawn lose their power and shatter or die, leaving Aether Essence behind.

AFTERWARD

Five rulers rise

Five people absorb Aether Essence, accidentally or deliberately, and become powerful rulers or beings associated with five main archipelagos.

THE MODERN WORLD

Old technology becomes rare

Most old technology stops working after the Divergence, leaving a future world with an old, medieval feel alongside ancient futuristic ruins.
