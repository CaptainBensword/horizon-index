CHARACTER / CAPTAIN

Blaise

A young captain who sets out across the world of Horizon.

CLASSIFICATIONCharacter
ROLECaptain
WEAPONSword
OVERVIEW

The Captain

Blaise is a young captain whose life is closely tied to the sea and the wider world. His journey takes him from the islands he knows into a much larger story.

APPEARANCE

Appearance

Blaise has red hair and greenish eyes. He commonly wears layered brown and black leather clothing over a lighter tunic, with a sword carried at his side.

EQUIPMENT

Sword

Blaise carries a sword as his primary weapon. His equipment and fighting style develop as his journey continues.

COMPANION

Matey

Blaise travels with Matey, a Cured Spawn parrot known for his relaxed and humorous personality.
