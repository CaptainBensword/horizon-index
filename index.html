ABOUT HORIZON

What is Horizon?

Horizon is a book series set in a future version of Earth, in a world shaped by events that happened roughly 2,000 years before Blaise's story.

Five giant beings known as the First Spawn emerged from the oceans and devastated civilization. In the aftermath, the world was left as a collection of archipelagos, and new creatures known as Spawn became part of the world's history.

The Horizon Index exists to collect the confirmed information behind that world — from its creatures and locations to its history, Aether, Relics, timeline, and books.
