HORIZON INDEX / AETHER

Aether

Aether appears in several distinct forms throughout the world, with different origins and uses.

AETHER / 01
◇

Aether Shards

Produced when normal Spawn are killed. They function as international currency and can power navigation and Spawn-tracking technology.

AETHER / 02
◇

Aether Essence

A different form of Aether left behind by the First Spawn. Five people absorbed it and became powerful rulers or beings associated with major archipelagos.

AETHER / 03
◇

Aether Cores

Rare cores found on certain isolated islands. A Core is required for a Relic to form, but people cannot deliberately manufacture a Relic from one.

The exact process by which an Aether Core produces a Relic remains mysterious.
