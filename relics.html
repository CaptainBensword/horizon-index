Relics

Mysterious semi-living objects connected to Aether Cores. Relics can take many forms, and some possess full minds of their own.

RELIC SYSTEM
Relics can form on islands with Aether Cores, including in water. The longer a Relic remains untouched, the stronger it can become. People mentally bond with Relics to access their full potential.
