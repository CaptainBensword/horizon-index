Relics

Mysterious objects formed around Aether Cores. Their forms, abilities, and minds can vary greatly.

RELIC OVERVIEW

What are Relics?

Relics are mysterious semi-living objects that can take forms such as weapons, jewelry, or other objects. They can appear on islands with an Aether Core, including in the water. The longer a Relic remains untouched, the stronger it can become. People can mentally bond with Relics to access their full potential, and some Relics are fully sentient.
