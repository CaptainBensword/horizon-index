Timeline

~2,000 YEARS BEFORE BLAISE

Earth before the First Spawn

Earth is essentially like present-day Earth.

THE FIRST SPAWN

Five gigantic beasts emerge

The First Spawn crawl from the oceans and devastate civilization.

THE DIVERGENCE

Earth becomes archipelagos

The 3rd Guardian tears Earth into islands and archipelagos.

AFTER THE FIRST SPAWN

Aether Essence remains

The First Spawn lose their power and leave Aether Essence behind.

THE FIVE RULERS

Essence is absorbed

Five people absorb Aether Essence and become powerful rulers or beings associated with major archipelagos.

THE PRESENT ERA

The Horizon world

Most ancient technology is gone, while Aether, Spawn, Relics, ships, and ruins remain part of the world.
