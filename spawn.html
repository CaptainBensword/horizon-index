HORIZON INDEX / SPAWN

Spawn Encyclopedia

Confirmed records of Spawn breeds currently documented in the Horizon world.

#001 · HEAVY SPAWN
◈

Bonebacks

Huge, heavy Spawn with insect-like proportions, rhino-like bulk and horn, and overlapping organic bone armor.

#002 · AQUATIC SPAWN
◈

Tideclaws

Aquatic Spawn with broad bodies and powerful clawed limbs, commonly found around rocky coastlines and shallow waters.

#003 · SWAMP SPAWN
◈

Mirewalkers

Long-legged swamp Spawn whose bodies remain above the water while their feet barely disturb the mud.

#004 · MINERAL-AREA SPAWN
◈

Metalmaws

Heavy cat-like predators with dark, rough skin and massive claws coated in metal or other minerals.

#005 · FLYING SPAWN
◈

Sky Rays

Huge manta-like flying Spawn that live high above the clouds and glide on air currents. They are harmless and known to help people.

#006 · FOREST SPAWN
◈

Bramblebacks

Low, heavily built Spawn with thick natural growths along their backs, associated with dense forests and vegetation.

#007 · FLYING SPAWN
◈

Glasswings

Lightweight flying Spawn with broad translucent wings, active around bright open areas.

#008 · UNDERGROUND SPAWN
◈

Burrowers

Stocky underground Spawn with reinforced heads and powerful digging limbs that create tunnels.

#009 · COLD-WEATHER SPAWN
◈

Frosthorns

Large cold-weather Spawn with thick hides and prominent horns, adapted to freezing environments and snow.

#010 · NOCTURNAL SPAWN
◈

Dusk Stalkers

Quiet nocturnal Spawn that rely heavily on their senses and are rarely seen in daylight.

#011 · DEEP-OCEAN SPAWN
◈

Deepmaw

A totally massive deep-ocean Spawn resembling a giant Venus flytrap. Its black form can swallow whole ships and is rarely seen clearly.

The Index records known breeds without filling gaps with unconfirmed species or alternate concepts.
