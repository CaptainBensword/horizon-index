Spawn Encyclopedia

Spawn records currently documented in the Horizon Index.

#001

Bonebacks

Large armored Spawn with overlapping organic bone plates, a heavy build, and horned heads.

→ #002

Tideclaws CONCEPT

Aquatic Spawn with broad bodies and powerful clawed limbs, commonly found around rocky coastlines and shallow waters.

→ #003

Mirewalkers

Long-legged swamp Spawn that move through soft mud with very little disturbance.

→ #004

Metalmaws

Heavy predators with dark, rough skin and large mineral-covered claws.

→ #005

Sky Rays

Huge manta-like flying Spawn that glide high above the clouds and are generally harmless.

→ #006

Bramblebacks CONCEPT

Low, heavily built Spawn with thick natural growths along their backs, associated with dense forests and vegetation.

→ #007

Glasswings CONCEPT

Lightweight flying Spawn with broad, translucent wings, active around bright open areas.

→ #008

Burrowers CONCEPT

Stocky underground Spawn with reinforced heads and powerful digging limbs that create extensive tunnels.

→ #009

Frosthorns CONCEPT

Large cold-weather Spawn with thick hides and prominent horns, adapted to freezing environments and snow.

→ #010

Dusk Stalkers CONCEPT

Quiet nocturnal Spawn that rely heavily on their senses and are rarely seen during daylight.
